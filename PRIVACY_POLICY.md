# Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 29, 2026

Private Notes Light is designed as a local-first notes app. The app does not require an account, does not use a backend server, and does not intentionally collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information.

## Information the App Stores

Private Notes Light stores app data locally on your device, including:

- Encrypted note content
- Note titles, which are stored in plaintext
- App settings, such as theme preferences
- Encrypted authentication and encryption metadata needed to unlock your notes

Your master password is not stored. It is used to derive an encryption key on your device.

## Note Encryption

Note content is encrypted before it is saved to local storage. The app uses your master password to unlock encryption material stored on the device.

Important limitation: note titles are not encrypted and are stored in plaintext. Avoid placing sensitive information in note titles.

## Local-Only Storage

Private Notes Light does not provide cloud sync, accounts, analytics, advertising, or server-side storage. Your notes remain on your device unless you choose to export, back up, share, or otherwise move them outside the app.

## Backups and Exports

If you export or back up your notes, you are responsible for where that exported file is stored and how it is protected. Files saved to cloud storage, shared with other apps, sent by email, or transferred to another device may be subject to the privacy practices of those services.

## Permissions

The app may request access to files or storage when you import or export note backups. Access is used only to complete the import or export action you choose.

## Third-Party Services

Private Notes Light does not integrate third-party analytics, advertising networks, or tracking services.

## Data Sharing

The app does not sell, rent, or share your personal information. Because the app does not intentionally collect personal information or transmit note data to a server, there is no server-side user data to disclose.

## Data Deletion

You can delete notes inside the app. You can also remove app data by uninstalling the app or clearing its local storage through your device settings. If you created exported backup files, those files must be deleted separately from wherever you stored them.

## Children's Privacy

Private Notes Light is not directed to children under 13. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be reflected by updating this file and the effective date above.

## Contact

For privacy questions, contact the app developer or maintainer through the project's published support or repository contact channels.
